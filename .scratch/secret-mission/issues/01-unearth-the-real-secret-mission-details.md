# 01 — Unearth the real Secret Mission details

## Question

What can we establish about the original game the player remembers — a stickman platformer (ladders, ropes, gaps) preinstalled on an Alcatel One Touch 311L, bought in Romania around 2001, remembered as "Secret Mission"? Specifically: the real title (was it Secret Mission, Stickman, or something else?), developer/publisher, the number of levels, screenshots of gameplay, and any description of mechanics. Sources to try: Romanian retro mobile forums (mygsm.ro, gsmmania.ro, phonegsm.ro and their Wayback snapshots), Alcatel user-manual PDFs for the OT-311/310/300 family, eBay/OLX/Vinted listings of the 311L with game-menu photos, 4PDA forum threads, old-game archives (old-games.ru, pro-j2me), and Wayback CDX for spec sites (gsmarena.com, phonemuseum.com, manualslib). Note: mainstream search engines are bot-blocked; prefer direct site/API probing, Wayback Machine, and CDX queries.

## Answer

The game is almost certainly **Run Run** — a Lode Runner-style platformer burned into the Alcatel One Touch 311/310 firmware (Romania 2001 fits the OT-311, announced 2001). Stickman hero climbing ladders, traversing ropes, digging trap holes, dodging chasing creatures, collecting items, exiting through a door — matches the player's memory. "Secret Mission" appears in no source; treat it as a misremembered title. The 311 shipped 3 games "on some versions" (Run Run, Killer Exp, Eyes & Star); the "311L" model doesn't exist. **Run Run's level count is not documented anywhere** — sources only say "many stages" (score caps at 999, save slots exist).
